About the shop

A deli with a cafe's patience.

Most delis rush the coffee. Most cafes buy the bread. We're building one counter that takes both halves seriously: the slicer and the espresso machine share the same bench, and the morning bake sets the day's schedule.

The shop is small — a dozen seats, a window bench, and a case you can see straight into. We'd rather sell out of six good sandwiches than keep thirty mediocre ones on a laminated menu.

We're finishing the buildout now. Where, exactly? That's the one thing we're keeping on the board inside until the paint dries — join the opening list below and you'll know before the sign goes up.
